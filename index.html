📖 Thoda Alag, Thoda Zyada

Mohd Affan Chaudhary

Main Haryana se hoon, par qismat mujhe yahan le aayi jahan main aaj hoon. Abhi main GD Goenka University se Pharmacy pursue kar raha hoon.

Zindagi ko main ek subject ki tarah nahi, ek experience ki tarah padhta hoon - jisme har din kuch naya seekhne ko milta hai.

Main apni life ko lightly nahi leta, par seriously enjoy zaroor karta hoon. Har pal ko jeena, usme thoda sa fun, thodi si shayari aur thoda sa khud ka rang milana - bas yahi mera style hai.

Main baaki logon ki tarah bilkul bhi nahi hoon - aur honestly, mujhe aisa rehna bahut pasand hai. Main crowd ka hissa banna nahi chahta, main apni hi duniya ka resident hoon.

Main zyada der upset reh hi nahi pata. Dil halka rakhta hoon, baatein gehri hoti hain par chehra aksar hasta hua hota hai. Shayad isi liye jo mujhe theek se jaante hain, wo kehte hain: "Tu bore ho hi nahi sakta."

Aur meri family? 💛 Sach bataun to meri family bhi boring nahi hai. Maine dekha hai kai gharon mein log saath hoke bhi bore rehte hain, par hamare yahan hansi apne aap chalti rehti hai. Mere ghar ke pal, aksar yaadon mein muskurate hue milte hain.

🌙 Bachpan Se Aaj Tak

Bachpan simple tha, par khushiyon se bhara hua. Chhoti chhoti baaton mein excitement, aur bina wajah hans dena - shayad wahi aadat aaj tak saath hai.

Waqt ke saath samajh aayi responsibilities, career ka pressure, aur future ke sawaal. Par ek baat kabhi nahi badli - life ko enjoy karne ka junoon.

Main maanta hoon zindagi perfect nahi hoti, par agar tum usse pyaar karna seekh lo na, to wo tumhare liye perfect ban jaati hai.

Pehli Mohabbat

It wasn't planned. Kabhi socha nahi tha ki pyaar itna real ho sakta hai.

Main chull mein relationship mein aaya. Bas timepass ke liye. Par tumne mujhe seriously le liya, aur main bhi tumhe seriously lene laga.

Efforts kiye maine. Dil se. Aur tumne bhi jawab diya. Aur ek din, hum relationship mein aa gaye. Officially.

Wo kahani zyada lambi nahi thi... sirf 2 mahine se bhi kam. Par khoobsurti length mein nahi hoti, ehsaas mein hoti hai. Aur wo ehsaas... abhi tak zinda hai.

Pehle Bakwaas Lagta Tha

Love wasn't something I believed in. Sach kahun, mujhe ye sab bakwaas lagta tha.

"Uski aankhon se pyaar hai" - main sochta tha yeh sirf filmon mein hota hai. "Use dekhe bina mann nahi lagta" - aise logon ka main mazaak bhi banata tha.

Aur phir... Zindagi ne mujhe hi wo lesson padha diya. Jab tumhe dekha, samajh aa gaya ki wo sab sach tha.

Tumhari aankhein. Tumhari muskurahat. Tumhari har baat. Sab kuch itna real tha ki main khud ko rok nahi paya. Girta chala gaya. Deep. Bahut deep.

Wo Achanak Aayi

My world changed completely. Tumse pehle aur tumse baad - do alag duniya.

Sach kahun, mujhe aaj bhi lagta hai aisa relationship shayad kisi ka nahi hoga. Jo humne share kiya, wo khaas tha. Bahut khaas.

Late night calls jo kabhi khatam hi nahi hoti thi. Coffee dates jahan sirf tum aur main the, duniya gaayab ho jaati thi. Wo smile jo dekh ke dil bhar aata tha.

Tumhari aankhein... Hayee... Astaghfirullah 😭 Usi mein kho jaana, usi mein jeena - yahi to pyaar tha. Tumhare saath har lamha ek khubsurat khwaab jaisa tha.

End Jo Aana Hi Tha

All good things come to an end, they say. Aur hamare saath bhi wahi hua.

Tumne breakup kar liya. Aur kisi aur ke relationship mein chali gayi. Jaldi mein. Bahut jaldi mein.

Theek hai. Sabki apni pasand hoti hai. Main tumhe khush dekhna chahta hoon. Hamesha.

Bas... ek baat thi jo galat lagi. Cheat karke jaana. Wo dard alag tha. Par main samajh gaya - shayad main kaafi nahi tha. Shayad tum better deserve karti thi.

Uske Baad

Uske baad se zindagi badal gayi. Trust issues ho gaye. Mujhe kisi ladki par bharosa hi nahi hua.

Friends bahut hain. Acche hain. Close bhi hain. Par relationship? Bas ek hi thi. Tumhare saath. Chhoti si. Par dil tak gayi.

Maine koshish ki move on karne ki. Logo se mile. Baat ki. Par koi bhi tumhari jagah nahi le sakaa.

Kyunki tum tum ho. Unique. Special. Aur koi tumhari tarah nahi ho sakta. Shayad isliye main abhi bhi atka hua hoon.

Aankhon Ka Sach

Ab samajh aata hai aankhon se pyaar kya hota hai. Tumne mujhe wo ehsaas diya jo kabhi nahi bhulta.

Sach hai - Jise tum pasand karte ho, uski har cheez se pyaar ho jaata hai. Uski aawaaz, uski hansi, uski choti choti aaadatein - sab kuch.

Main is trap mein fas gaya. Tumhare trap mein. Aur aaj tak nikal nahi paya. Shayad nikalna bhi nahi chahta.

Kyunki tumhare saath bitaye wo pal, wo memories - wo sab itne qeemti hain ki main unhe chodna nahi chahta. Chahe dard ho, chahe sukoon - wo sab tumhare hain.

Kami

Jaise paani ki kami sharab poori nahi kar sakti, waise hi tumhari kami koi aur poori nahi kar sakta.

Options ki kami nahi hai. Log kehte hain - "Aage badh, move on kar, bahut log hain duniya mein." Par bhai... tumhari aankhen.

Tumhare saath jo connection tha, wo dobara kisi ke saath nahi ban sakta. Main jaanta hoon. Try kiya hai maine. Par har baar fail hua.

Tumhari yaad aati hai. Raat ko. Din mein. Har waqt. Coffee peete hue tumhara khyal. Gaane sunte hue tumhari awaaz. Sab jagah tum ho. Har jagah.

Khamosh Mohabbat

Aaj bhi main tumse utna hi pyaar karta hoon. Ye jaante hue bhi ke tum kisi aur ke saath ho. Ke tum kisi aur ko pasand karti ho.

Bewakoofi hai. Main jaanta hoon. Par dil ka sach bhi hai. Dil wo nahi suunta jo dimaag kehta hai.

Maine kabhi tumhe pareshan nahi kiya breakup ke baad. Aur na kabhi karunga. Tumhari respect kaafi hai mere liye. Tumhari khushi kaafi hai.

Bas door se dekhta rehta hoon. Social media pe scroll karte hue, kahin tumhari photo mil jaaye. Wo bhi bahut hai mere liye.

Uski Khushi

Tumhari khushi mein hi meri khushi hai. Ye dialogue nahi, hakeeqat hai. Main tumhe kabhi pareshan nahi karna chahta.

Agar tum kisi aur ke saath khush ho, to main bhi khush hoon. Dard hota hai, par accept kar liya hai maine.

Bas itna chahta hoon ki tum hamesha muskurati raho. Chahe main tumhari zindagi mein na bhi rahun, par tumhare chehre pe wo smile hamesha rahe.

Khamosh mohabbat. Ek taraffa ehsaas. Par saccha. Bilkul saccha. Aur shayad yahi sabse bada pyaar hai - jahan kuch mangna nahi, bas dena hota hai.

Aakhri Ehsaas

Mujhe pata hai tum shayad kabhi mujhe dekhna bhi pasand na karo. Shayad mera naam sunke bhi uncomfortable feel ho.

Par phir bhi... main tumse pyaar karta hoon. Beinteha. Beintehaan. Jo shabd mein nahi samjhaya ja sakta.

Kaash tum aao... ek baar. Aur sirf itna bolo: "Affan, main samajh gayi. Main jaanti hoon tum kitna pyaar karte the."

Bas. Itna kaafi hai. Phir main chain se jee sakta hoon. Phir mera dil sukoon se reh sakta hai.
